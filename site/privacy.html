Privacy Policy

Rien Ne Va Plus · effective 25 October 2026

Rien Ne Va Plus collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Rien Ne Va Plus is a single-player game made by Gabe, its developer (“we”). This policy covers the game on Android and its web version.

What the game keeps, and where

To let you carry on where you left off, the game saves a few things on your device only:

  • your run in progress and what you have unlocked (chip sets, the Vault, House Edge, the Almanac and your lifetime statistics);
  • your settings (sound levels, text size, graphics, vibration and tips);
  • images the game draws for itself, such as the floor and the people in the hall, kept so it starts faster.

None of this identifies you, and none of it is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash-reporting services, or include third-party trackers.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer data, because it has none to share.
  • It does not ask for any dangerous permissions. On Android it uses vibration only, for the buzz when the ball hops or a chip wins, and you can turn that off in Settings.

Backups

If you have turned on Android's own backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the game's saves in your device backup, so they come back when you reinstall or move to a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under your Google account and its terms; we never receive it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything it saved. In the web version, clearing the site's data in your browser does the same.

Children

The game simulates casino gambling with play money that has no value outside the game, and it is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Purchases

If you buy the game on Google Play, the purchase is handled entirely by Google. We do not receive your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Because the game collects nothing, a change could only ever add a clear explanation of something new before it happens.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the game: open an issue at github.com/RattleSnake06/RouletteGame/issues.
